# Warm Counter Design System & Restrained Visual Hierarchy

> **Status: Superseded by [ADR 0012](./0012-street-food-mafia-brand-theme.md)**

High-stress restaurant counter operations demand high-contrast legibility, tactile confidence, and zero visual distraction during peak rushes. We selected the **Warm Counter** theme—featuring warm neutral page backgrounds (`#F5F3EE` light, `#151A17` dark), deep forest green primary actions (`#245C40` light, `#9ED3AC` dark), clean Inter/Segoe UI typography with tabular numerals, 48px minimum touch targets, and restrained flat cards without gradients, glassmorphism, or heavy drop shadows.

This design eliminates eye fatigue under fluorescent kitchen lighting, guarantees rapid finger tapping on counter touchscreens and laptop trackpads, ensures immediate numeral comparison for order totals and cash change, and maintains a strict visual contract where deep green denotes primary actions and selected states while surrounding surfaces remain calm and neutral.
