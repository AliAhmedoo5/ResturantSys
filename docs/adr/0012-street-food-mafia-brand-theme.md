# Street-Food / Mafia Visual Brand Design System

## Context
ADR 0007 established the legacy "Warm Counter" design system with terracotta and warm amber tones. v8 Chapter 34 mandates the authoritative production aesthetic: the Street-Food / Mafia branding theme, featuring a high-contrast dark palette tailored for dense counter readability and 1366x768 non-scrolling touch/keyboard ergonomics.

## Decision
We supersede ADR 0007 and adopt the Chapter 34 Street-Food / Mafia palette:
- Obsidian Black (`#050708`): primary application background
- Dark Navy (`#101820`): fixed compact sidebar and deepest surfaces
- Cartel Yellow (`#FFC400`): primary CTAs and active navigation markers
- Burnt Gold (`#F5A623`): secondary accents and subtle styling
- Off White (`#F5F7FA`): primary legible typography
- Card Surface (`#14242D`): cards, dialogs, and dense data tables

## Consequences
- Replaces Warm Counter colors with the dark, high-contrast palette.
- High focus affordance for touch and keyboard shortcuts.
- Preserves 1366x768 screen efficiency without page-level scrolling.
