# Automatic Kitchen Void Slip on Post-Print Cancellation

**Status: Superseded by the v8 no-KOT policy.**

The v8 requirements exclude kitchen tickets, kitchen printers, kitchen stations, and kitchen queues. Staff communicate preparation changes verbally and the Cashier records fulfillment milestones. The user reaffirmed this policy on 2026-10-24.

Superseded further on 2026-10-26 by the user decision to remove all physical printing. Checkout records the order and shows its confirmation digitally; there are no customer receipt printers, cash-drawer pulses, print queues, or printer setup requirements. Cancellations remain audited and follow the stock-disposition and refund rules; they do not emit a kitchen void slip. The old kitchen generators, print-job types, queue controller, screens, navigation, and feature toggles have been removed.
