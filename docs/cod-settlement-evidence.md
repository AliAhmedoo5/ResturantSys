# COD settlement receiving evidence

Rider Trip Settlement remains a Cashier-only operation in a Cashier-owned open receiving Shift within the trip Business Day. The originating Order and dispatch Shift are unchanged. Settled Trip Lock continues to prevent direct tender, line and cancellation changes. Repeated or concurrent settlement submissions cannot create another deposit.

New settlements atomically append payment evidence using the trip ID as the stable settlement identity. Each delivered Collection Due Order gets an appended remaining-balance tender and a linked Order event. Existing partial receipts and their immutable event links remain intact. Direct digital confirmation produces a BANK receipt with the retained DIGITAL_WALLET tender type, displayed as Direct digital, and no physical deposit. Public Order reads and receiving-Shift electronic tender totals preserve this noncash type. Raw digital reference text stays in operational tender storage and is excluded from Manager analytics/exports.

A cash pouch is recorded once as a RIDER_HANDOVER event linked to its PAY_IN Cash Movement. Its amount is the counted physical cash, including any shortage or surplus, and is never allocated across individual Orders. The COD_SETTLEMENT Order event describes the remaining contractual amount settled under the existing settlement policy; its RIDER_UNVERIFIED source is not verified customer money or a second drawer inflow. The shared settlement ID connects these distinct facts without pretending the counted pouch proves which customers paid the rider.

Actual settlement/handover time and immutable recording time are separate. Immediate entry defaults actual time to recording time. Optional late actual time must fall within the authorized receiving Shift and Business Day and cannot be in the future. Known timestamps display in Asia/Karachi with original UTC evidence retained in source details and exports. This feature does not authorize closed-period correction entries or rewrite sealed reports.

Managers find receiving events in Delivery and riders and Cash and shifts. Rows show originating Order Business Day/Shift, receiving Business Day/Shift, Shift-owner Cashier, recorded-by identity, times, trip/settlement ID, tender ID and movement ID where applicable. Receiving Shift/Cashier/time filters select these event rows independently of the original Order cohort. Whole-drawer reconciliation retains its disclosed Business Day scope. CSV, XLSX and PDF serialize the same immutable source rows and definitions.

Delivered status, expected pouch value and settlement attribution do not establish verified customer-to-rider collection. That evidence remains explicitly unavailable. Legacy settled trips without retained events have unknown receiving Shift, recording actor and immutable recording time; no receiving attribution or actual money date is backfilled from the originating Order or a similarly sized Cash Movement. Their original records remain visible as operational context.
