# Historical correction reporting

Managers open **Historical corrections** from analytics, select a closed Business Day and choose Original sealed or Correction-adjusted. The original sealed JSON, its hash and original recording timestamp are retained separately. The adjustment view uses an inclusive UTC recording cutoff and shows original values, individual corrections and adjusted values. CSV, Excel and PDF export the displayed immutable snapshot and cutoff.

A signed-in, active Manager must verify their own PIN for every append or contra-reversal. Cashier sessions cannot read or append corrections. Each correction requires signed Integer Paisa, an actual Event Time, a source evidence reference, a reason of at least ten characters, and explicit confirmation that the physical effect has been reconciled against the original evidence and was not posted elsewhere. Unknown historical Event Times are not inferred: obtain evidence before appending a financial correction.

Late Collection Due receipts identify the original Order and cannot exceed its unreconciled debt. Drawer disbursements identify an affected closed Shift in the selected Business Day. External payments are separately identified and never adjust drawer variance. Physical evidence references cannot be reused, including after reversal; replacements use a new reference explicitly linking the reversed correction. SQLite rejects updates, deletes and a second reversal. A contra-reversal copies the original attribution and reverses its exact signed amount.

No command changes original Orders, Business Days, Shifts, balances, payment events, cash movements, or sealed X/Z reports. A late supplier payout explains the original shortage; a late COD receipt reduces correction-adjusted Collection Due. These are administrative views, not live tender collection or supplier ledger settlement. External financial effects remain itemized evidence rather than invented drawer effects.

Coverage: `test/analytics/historical_corrections_test.dart` exercises real temporary SQLite, authorized commands, original versus adjusted variance, duplicate rejection, reversal, cutoff and export metadata. `test/widgets/historical_corrections_workspace_test.dart` protects the Manager interface. All Flutter analysis and tests run exclusively in GitHub Actions. Original sealed Shift rows carry missing counts as unknown; correction reporting preserves those gaps rather than assigning zero.

Source references are compared without case or repeated whitespace differences. Optional original evidence links must resolve; retained physical postings cannot be appended again. Unrecorded slips require a unique retained physical reference and explicit reconciliation attestation. Distinct effects with different source identities remain separate even when their amounts and Event Times match. Missing sealed summary rows remain unknown; retained Collection Due can reconcile independently of an unavailable original cash baseline. Exports retain schema version and the historical-corrections-v1 report version.
